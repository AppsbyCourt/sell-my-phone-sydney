How fast do I actually get paid? +
If you drop in to our CBD office, you'll usually walk out with a PayID confirmation within 15 minutes. Pickups same-day across Sydney metro, paid as soon as we've inspected.
What if my screen is cracked? +
We still buy it — we just adjust the offer to match. Tick "Has issues" on the form and tell us what's going on. We'd rather know upfront than surprise you at handover.

Phones still on a carrier plan or finance — sorry, not yet. They need to be fully paid off and carrier-unlocked. We'll help you check if you're not sure.
