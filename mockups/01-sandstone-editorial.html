§ 03 — Answers

Common questions.

If yours isn’t here, text the office on 0488 SYD PHN. A human will reply, usually within the hour.

Q.01

How quickly will I get paid?

Same day, every day. Drop-offs in the CBD are settled by PayID before you leave reception. Metro pickups are paid the moment our technician confirms condition — usually within ten minutes of arrival.

Q.02

What if the screen is cracked?

Still worth quoting. We buy phones in every condition — even water-damaged or non-booting. The quote simply reflects the work needed. We’ll tell you exactly what we’re deducting and why.

Q.03

Do I need the box and charger?

Not at all. They add a small premium — typically $20–$40 on flagship handsets — but the device alone is enough. Locked, iCloud-locked, or carrier-locked phones can’t be purchased.

Q.04

Where in Sydney are you?

Our office sits a two-minute walk from Wynyard, on the third floor of a sandstone building on York Street. Free pickup runs across the CBD, Inner West, Eastern Suburbs, Lower North Shore, and Parramatta.
