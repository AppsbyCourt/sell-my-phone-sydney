FILE / 005 — REFERENCE FAQ · 04 ENTRIES
↳ Frequently asked

Questions, plainly answered.

Still got questions? Call the workshop on 02 9123 4567 — you'll get a real human who can quote your phone over the phone.

Q.001
How do you decide what my phone is worth?

Our quote engine pulls live AU resale data — eBay completed listings, Swappa AU, local trade-in benchmarks — and adjusts for the condition you select. We re-grade on inspection and only deduct for damage you didn't disclose, with photos shown to you first.

Q.002
What if my phone has a cracked screen or other issues?

We still buy it. Select "Has issues" and describe the fault — most cracked-screen iPhones from the last four years still fetch $180 – $620. We'd rather give you an honest reduced price than refuse the device.

Q.003
How fast do I actually get paid?

Walk-in: paid before you leave the workshop, usually within 15 minutes via PayID. Pickup: paid the same day, once our courier returns to Marrickville and the inspection clears. No 5-day "processing window" nonsense.

Q.004
Do you wipe my data, and is that documented?

Yes. Every handset goes through a NIST SP 800-88 compliant erase on a sealed bench. You'll receive a signed wipe certificate by email within 48 hours of sale, and we never resell a device that still has account locks attached.
