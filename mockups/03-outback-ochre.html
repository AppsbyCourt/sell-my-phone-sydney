Q1

How fast do I actually get paid?

If you walk into our Pitt Street shopfront with a phone that matches the quote, you're walking back out with PayID in your bank in under an hour — usually closer to 25 minutes. Metro pickups run same day if you book before 2pm.

Q2

Do you buy cracked or locked phones?

Yes — both. Cracked glass, dud batteries, dead boards, iCloud locked, carrier locked. Just be straight in the form. We'll quote a fair parts-or-repair price and stand behind it. Honest write-ups get the highest offers, every time.

+
Q3

Is meeting in person safe?

Our Pitt Street shopfront is open Mon–Sat with two staff on every shift, CCTV throughout and an Australia Post counter two doors down for ID checks. If you'd rather we come to you, our runners only meet at offices, cafés or public lobbies — never carparks or homes.

+
Q4

What happens to my data?

Every phone is wiped to the manufacturer's factory-reset spec the second it crosses our counter, then verified on a second pass before resale. You'll get a wipe certificate emailed within 24 hours with the IMEI and timestamp — keep it for your records.
