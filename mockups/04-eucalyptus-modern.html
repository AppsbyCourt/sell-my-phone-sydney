Questions

A few things people often ask.

Still wondering? Send us a message or call the Sydney CBD office on (02) 8005 4471 — a real human picks up.

How do you decide the price?
We benchmark every model against current resale data across the AU market — eBay sold listings, refurb wholesale, and our own resale history. Then we work backwards from the condition you've described. The quote you see is the price we pay.
Where in Sydney do you come to?
Anywhere within the Sydney metro at no extra cost — CBD, Eastern Suburbs, Inner West, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills, Western Sydney, Sutherland Shire. For Wollongong, Newcastle or the Central Coast, get in touch.
What if the phone is locked or has issues?
We still buy phones with cracked screens, dead batteries, water damage, or activation locks — the price just reflects the condition. Pick "Has issues" on the form and tell us what's going on; we'll quote on it honestly.
Will the price change when you arrive?
Only if what we find is materially different from what you described — for example, "Like new" but actually with a deep crack. In that case we'll show you the updated quote on the spot. No pressure, no hard sell. If you'd rather not sell, we drive away.

Ready when you are.

Tell us what you've got and we'll be back with a firm quote, usually within 10 minutes. No accounts, no spam.
